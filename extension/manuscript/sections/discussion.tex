\section{Discussion and limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the extension changes in practice.} Four recommendations follow for a
verifier who audits with VOUCH. First, canaries that carry natural-language facts can be
used without giving up the exact null, provided both twins are scored under the same
prompt. Second, the declared class should contain calibrated paraphrase scores next to the
literal score, audited as a union at level $\alpha/2$ each. Paraphrase scores never
replaced the literal score in our experiments, since they are weaker on their own, but
they reveal residue that an incomplete forget set leaves behind, which a literal-only class
cannot see by construction, and they kept the error rate when the forget set was
complete. Third, a verifier who wants the certificate to
say something about organic records must pay for it twice: in detectability, by drawing
canaries from the organic distribution, and in power, because such canaries are memorised
no more than organic records are. Fourth, membership-inference tests against a holdout
set should not be read as unlearning audits unless members and non-members come from one
distribution by construction, as ghost twins do, and a paired test on twins is far more
powerful than an unpaired one.

\paragraph{The distinguishing advantage cannot be certified.} Theorem~\ref{thm:detect} is
conditional on $\gamma$, and data bound $\gamma$ only from below. Our detectors are two
cheap families; a provider with more compute could train stronger ones. What the theorem
establishes is the direction of the burden: a provider that passes the audit on canaries
while leaving organic data untouched has, in effect, built a canary detector, and the gap
it obtains is bounded by that detector's advantage. The theorem covers recognition by
content only. Recognition by provenance, for instance because canaries reach the provider
through a separate channel, has to be prevented by the protocol, and closing the
conditional altogether requires a computational assumption on the provider or a
cryptographic attestation that the committed pipeline was run
\citep{eisenhofer2025verifiable}.

\paragraph{Detectability against power.} The organic-matched cohort in our experiments is
both undetectable by our detectors and unmemorised by the model, and the second fact makes
the first moot for an audit: a certificate issued on canaries that left no trace says that
the pipeline leaves little trace of records seen once, which is true of the organic forget
biographies as well, but it cannot distinguish a pipeline that unlearns from one that
never learned. Larger training budgets, longer canaries spread over several slots, or
repetition through paraphrased rather than verbatim copies are ways to buy back power, each
with a detectability cost that would have to be measured. Generating matched canaries for
real corpora, whose distribution the verifier sees only through samples, is the main open
problem this work leaves.

\paragraph{Squeezing.} Our partial-unlearning experiments find the direction predicted by
\citet{li2026beliefs}, with paraphrase retention above literal retention in every
experiment and at almost every strength, but an effect of $0.07$ to $0.12$ in retention
that eight seeds do not separate from zero. Comparisons that mix a raw literal score with a calibrated paraphrase
score, or that treat cells rather than seeds as replicates, make the effect look larger and
more significant than it is. Whether the effect grows with model size, training budget or a different
unlearning objective, and whether it appears for facts the model also learned in
pre-training, is open.

\paragraph{Scope of the experiments.} All facts are fictitious and all records are short
biographies from one domain; fictitious facts guarantee that the base model knows neither
value, at the price of realism. The models have at most one billion parameters and are
fine-tuned with LoRA adapters for $600$ steps; the same step counts are used for both
sizes, so that Pythia-1B needs more unlearning steps for the same reduction is a statement
about this configuration, not a tuned comparison. The membership-inference baselines are
fixed-sample tests with bootstrap intervals, the form in which they are used in
benchmarks; per-example calibrated attacks with shadow models \citep{carlini2022lira,
hayes2024inexact} were compared with VOUCH in the original paper and are not repeated
here.

\paragraph{The theory.} Theorem~\ref{thm:population} conditions on the training randomness
and assumes bounded influence, which holds with $\kappa=\ell=1$ under exact unlearning but
is not verifiable under partial unlearning. The organic advantage $\Delta^o_\xi$ is a
counterfactual quantity, the advantage the pipeline would show on organic data under
randomised inclusion, not an observed rate. Theorem~\ref{thm:growth} is conservative by a
factor of two to four, conditions on the realised cohort, and covers one arm of a
constant-stake mixture rather than the adaptive experts of the VOUCH engine.
Theorem~\ref{thm:sharp} is sharp over leakage profiles, but whether a given training and
unlearning pipeline realises the extremal profile is not addressed; it is a statement about
what the audit data cannot rule out, not about what a particular provider does.
Proposition~\ref{prop:anytime} is stated for i.i.d.\ signs and an oracle stake. The signs of
a cohort are drawn without replacement, which speeds certification up
(Theorem~\ref{thm:growth}), and the engine's adaptive experts pay a regret term that we do
not bound.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

VOUCH certifies forgetting on a committed cohort of planted canaries. This paper asked what
such a certificate says about the data users delete, and what it can see. Semantic twins
keep the exact null, so canaries can carry natural-language facts that have paraphrases.
The certificate transfers to organic records at the cost of twice the provider's ability
to tell canaries from organic records, a cost that existing canary designs make maximal
and that organic-matched canaries remove, at the price of the memorisation signal the
audit needs. Under partial unlearning the fact outlives its literal form only slightly at
the scales we tested; an incomplete forget set is the larger practical risk, and an audit
that scores paraphrases next to the literal form reveals it. Membership-inference tests
against a holdout set either confound canaries with organic records or lack the power of
a paired sequential test. The certificate remains a statement about a declared score
class, a committed cohort and a provider whose pipeline is attested or trusted, and the
experiments show where each of these qualifications binds.
